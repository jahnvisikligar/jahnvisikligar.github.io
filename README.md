## About the Website

The website brings together my experience, projects, learning, and
professional interests in one place. It is designed to make my work and
career story easy to explore, with dedicated pages for my journey,
projects, recognition, writing, and collaboration.

## Website Sections

-   **Home** --- an introduction to my professional focus and selected highlights.
-   **My Professional Journey** --- career experience, education, and professional growth.
-   **Let's Collab / Work With Me** --- collaboration interests and ways to get in touch.
-   **Contact** --- contact details and relevant links.

*Section names and routes should be kept aligned with the actual files and navigation in the repository.*

## Design Direction

The visual direction is clean, editorial, and professional. The design system is being refined around a consistent colour palette and shared
hero styling across pages.

### Current Brand Colours

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Colour                  Hex                     Intended use
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Primary blue            `#244A63`               Primary actions, links, and key accents
  
  Deep blue               `#203F56`               Strong headings and high-emphasis elements
  
  Pale blue               `#E6F0F2`               Soft highlights, cards, and supporting surfaces
  
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The remaining neutral colours are still being finalized.

### Typography and Layout Consistency

The `lets-collab.html` page is the reference for hero typography and
spacing. Other pages, including `career.html` and `contact.html`, should
follow its established heading styles, line heights, alignment, and
vertical spacing.

Design goals: - Consistent hero typography and spacing across pages. -
Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile. - Reusable styling
and minimal duplicated CSS. - Clear hierarchy, readable content, and
restrained use of colour. - Preserve each page's content and
functionality while improving visual consistency.
